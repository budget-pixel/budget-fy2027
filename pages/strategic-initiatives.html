Introduction and Overview

Strategic Initiatives

Board priorities translated into measurable work

A clear line from community priorities to departmental results

The Board of County Commissioners establishes strategic initiatives to guide policy, investment, and service delivery. Board departments connect their annual goals to those initiatives, define measurable objectives, and report performance so residents can see how the County’s work supports its long-term direction.
