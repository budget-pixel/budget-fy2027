Consolidated Financial Schedules

Below are the consolidated revenue and expenditure schedules for all appropriated funds. Property taxes are shown separately from other General Government Taxes. Mosquito Control is presented as a separate fund immediately after the Capital Projects Fund, with the General Fund and Mosquito Control Fund millage rates displayed for Florida Truth in Millage reporting.

Revenues are classified in accordance with the State of Florida’s Uniform Accounting System. Expenditures are presented by functional area, offering a comprehensive summary of the County’s financial operations and resource allocation.
