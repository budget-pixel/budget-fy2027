# Salary Guide

## Purpose

This document explains what compensation information this site does and does not publish, and where to find it.

## Overview

This site publishes **aggregate** personnel and compensation data — total Personnel Services cost by department and fund, and position/staffing counts through the Summary of Personnel page. It does not publish individual employee salary records. See [Personnel](personnel.md) for how Personnel Services costs are categorized and what drives them.

## What Determines Compensation

- **Board-governed departments**: pay structures and compensation plans are set through County policy, administered centrally by Human Resources, and subject to Board budget appropriation.
- **Constitutional Officers**: each office (Sheriff, Clerk of Courts, Property Appraiser, Supervisor of Elections, Tax Collector) sets its own hiring, position structure, and compensation decisions independently within its approved total budget appropriation. See [Constitutional Officers](constitutional-officers.md).
- **State-mandated components**: FICA (Social Security/Medicare) contributions and Florida Retirement System (FRS) contribution rates are set by federal and state law, not County discretion.

## Where to Find Specific Compensation Information

- **Aggregate figures**: Summary of Personnel (live page) on this site.
- **Individual public employee compensation records**: these are public records in Florida but are not published on this budget transparency site; requests should be directed to the relevant department, Constitutional Officer's office, or the County Clerk's public records function.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I look up what a specific County employee is paid on this site?** No — this site shows aggregate Personnel Services totals and position counts, not individual compensation records.

**Are Florida public employee salaries public record?** Generally yes, under Florida's public records law, but such records are not compiled or published on this budget site — a public records request to the relevant office is the appropriate channel.

**Does the Board set Sheriff's Office employee pay?** No — the Sheriff, as an independent Constitutional Officer, sets compensation for Sheriff's Office employees within the office's total appropriated budget.

## Common Misconceptions

- **"This site should show individual salaries."** This site is a budget transparency tool showing aggregate figures; individual compensation records are handled through Florida's separate public records request process.
- **"All County employees are paid under one unified pay scale."** Constitutional Officers set their own compensation structures independently from Board-governed departments.
