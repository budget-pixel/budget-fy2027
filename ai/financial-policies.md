# Financial Policies

## Purpose

This document summarizes Walton County's adopted financial policies — the rules that govern how the County manages reserves, prepares budgets, handles cash, administers grants, and safeguards public assets. Each summary here reflects the County's own published policy summary; the full adopted policy documents are linked from each section.

## Overview

The Board of County Commissioners has adopted financial policies to promote fiscal stability, transparency, accountability, and compliance with Florida law. These policies govern how the budget is built, monitored, and amended, and how the County protects public funds and assets.

## Fund Balance Policy

Establishes guidelines for maintaining adequate fund balances to support financial stability, liquidity, emergency preparedness, investment-grade credit ratings, and long-term fiscal sustainability. The policy defines the County's fund balance classifications and establishes reserve targets designed to protect the County's financial position. See [Reserves](reserves.md).

## Budget Policy

Provides the framework for preparing, adopting, and administering a balanced budget in accordance with Florida law. The policy promotes long-term planning, fiscal accountability, and responsible allocation of County resources. See [Budget Process](budget-process.md).

## Cash Handling Policy

Establishes internal controls and accountability measures for the receipt, safeguarding, deposit, and management of County funds to protect public assets and reduce financial risk.

## Grants Administration

Provides guidance for the administration, monitoring, and reporting of grant-funded activities to ensure compliance with applicable grant requirements and maximize the effective use of external funding sources. See [Grants](grants.md).

## Budget Transfers and Amendments

Establishes procedures for budget transfers and amendments in accordance with Florida Statutes, ensuring transparency, fiscal control, and appropriate authorization of budget changes. See [Budget Process](budget-process.md) for the statutory amendment process under Section 129.06, Florida Statutes.

## Capital Asset Policy

Provides standards for the acquisition, capitalization, inventory, maintenance, and disposal of County assets to ensure accountability and effective management of public resources. See [Expenditure Guide](expenditure-guide.md) (Capital Outlay).

## Anti-Fraud and Whistleblower Policies

Promotes ethical conduct, transparency, and accountability by establishing procedures for reporting suspected fraud, waste, abuse, or misconduct, and protecting individuals who report concerns in good faith.

## Investment Policy

Establishes guidelines for investing County funds with emphasis on safety, liquidity, diversification, and yield, while preserving principal and complying with Florida law.

## Indirect Administrative Cost Allocation Policy

Provides a methodology for allocating indirect administrative costs among County programs and funding sources, ensuring equitable cost recovery and compliance with applicable federal and state requirements. This is why departments funded by special revenue funds (e.g. Tourist Development Fund departments) show an "Indirect Administrative Fees" line — it reimburses the General Fund for the shared administrative services (HR, IT, finance, legal) those departments use.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why does Walton County maintain fund balance reserves instead of spending everything each year?**
Reserves support financial stability, liquidity for cash-flow timing, emergency preparedness (including hurricane response), investment-grade credit ratings (which lower the County's borrowing costs), and long-term fiscal sustainability. See [Common Misconceptions](common-misconceptions.md) and [Reserves](reserves.md).

**What happens if the County needs to change the budget mid-year?**
The Budget Transfers and Amendments policy and Section 129.06, Florida Statutes provide the formal process for the Board to amend the budget by resolution — see [Budget Process](budget-process.md).

**How does the County make sure grant money is spent correctly?**
The Grants Administration policy governs monitoring, administration, and reporting of grant-funded activities to ensure compliance with each grant's specific requirements.

**What is the Indirect Administrative Cost Allocation Policy, in plain terms?**
It's the methodology for fairly charging departments and funds for their share of countywide administrative services (like HR, IT, accounting, and legal support) that the General Fund provides but that specific funds/departments benefit from — so a special revenue fund isn't getting "free" administrative support paid for entirely by property taxpayers.

## Common Misconceptions

- **"Reserves are unused money the County is sitting on."** Reserves are a policy-driven, statutorily consistent safeguard for financial stability and emergency response, not idle funds — see [Reserves](reserves.md).
- **"Indirect Administrative Fees are a hidden or made-up charge."** They reflect a documented, policy-based cost allocation methodology ensuring each fund pays its fair share of shared administrative services.

## Definitions

- **Fund Balance**: the equity (assets minus liabilities) of a governmental fund — the formal accounting term for what is often informally called a fund's reserves.
- **Balanced Budget**: a budget in which appropriated expenditures do not exceed available revenues and fund balance.
