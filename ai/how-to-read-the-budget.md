# How to Read Walton County's Budget Tables

## Purpose

This document explains how to correctly interpret the figures shown across this site's budget tables and charts — what each column means, how rows roll up into totals, and the distinctions that matter most when citing a number.

## Overview

Nearly every financial table on this site follows the same column pattern, moving from historical actuals on the left to the current-year proposed budget on the right:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| FY 2020–2025 Actual | What the County actually collected or spent that fiscal year, based on closed-out financial records. |
| FY 2026 Budget | The original budget the Board adopted for the current fiscal year (FY 2026), before any mid-year amendments. |
| FY 2027 Proposed | The proposed budget for the upcoming fiscal year — the subject of this site. |

**Actual figures are historical fact; Budget and Proposed figures are financial plans.** A department's FY2025 Actual spending will not exactly match its FY2025 Budget, because actual conditions (staffing vacancies, project timing, emergency response, revenue performance) always differ somewhat from what was planned. That gap is normal and expected — see [Common Misconceptions](common-misconceptions.md) for why it does not indicate a problem.

## Actual vs. Budget vs. Proposed vs. Adopted

- **Actual**: the closed, historical record of what was truly collected or spent in a completed fiscal year.
- **Original Budget**: the budget the Board formally adopted for a given fiscal year, before any amendments.
- **Proposed Budget**: the budget presented for review before formal adoption — this is what "FY 2027" figures on this site represent until the Board completes the [budget process](budget-process.md) and adopts a final budget.
- **Adopted Budget**: the final budget the Board approves after public hearings, which becomes the fiscal year's Original Budget going forward.

## How Rows Roll Up

Revenue and expenditure tables group individual accounts into broader categories before reaching a grand total:

- **Revenue categories** (see [Revenue Guide](revenue-guide.md)): General Government Taxes, Permits Fees and Special Assessments, Intergovernmental Revenues, Charges for Services, Judgments Fines and Forfeits, Miscellaneous Revenue, Other Sources.
- **Expenditure categories** (see [Expenditure Guide](expenditure-guide.md)): Personnel Services, Operating Expenditures, Capital Outlay, Debt Service, Grants and Aid, Other Uses.
- **Expenditure activities**: broader functional groupings such as General Government, Public Safety, Physical Environment, Transportation, Economic Environment, Human Services, Culture and Recreation, Court-Related Cost, and Other Uses.

A table's **Subtotal** row sums every account within one category or activity; the **Total** row sums every subtotal. When a "View Budget Lines" detail is available, it shows the individual accounts underneath a subtotal so a reader can trace a summary figure back to its source line items.

## Fund Scoping

Many of the same revenue and expenditure categories appear on multiple tables at different scopes:

- **Countywide summaries** (Summary of Revenues, Summary of Expenses) combine every fund together.
- **Fund Financial Schedules** show the same categories scoped to one specific fund (e.g. the General Fund, or the Transportation Fund) or a group of funds.

A department or revenue line can appear on more than one table because it is being shown at a different scope — this is not double-counting. See [Fund Structure](fund-structure.md) for why the County separates resources into funds in the first place.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why don't Actual and Budget figures match for the same year?**
Actuals reflect what really happened; Budget reflects what was planned. Vacancies, project delays, emergency spending, weather events, and revenue performance all cause real-world variance from a plan set months in advance.

**What does "FY 2027 Proposed" mean, exactly?**
It is the budget Walton County has proposed for the fiscal year beginning October 1, 2026 and ending September 30, 2027, prior to final adoption through the process described in [Budget Process](budget-process.md).

**If a number changes between when I looked at this site last week and today, is that an error?**
Not necessarily. Budget development is iterative during the proposal and workshop phases, and figures can be refined before final adoption. Always note the date you're citing a figure and prefer the live page over a cached figure.

**Why do some tables show a project or account for multiple years?**
Multi-year capital projects and grant-funded programs are budgeted across the years they're expected to be active — see [Capital Projects](capital-projects.md).

## Common Misconceptions

- **"A budget number is a guarantee of exact spending."** A budget is an authorized spending ceiling and financial plan, not a prediction of the exact dollar that will be spent.
- **"If Actual is higher than Budget, something went wrong."** It can simply mean a revenue source outperformed projections, or an amendment authorized additional spending — see [Budget Process](budget-process.md) on amendments.
- **"Every dollar shown on a summary table is available to spend on anything."** Many revenue sources and funds are legally restricted — see [Fund Structure](fund-structure.md) and [Revenue Guide](revenue-guide.md).

## Definitions

See [Budget Terminology](budget-terminology.md) for the full glossary-linked list. Key terms used throughout this document:

- **Fiscal Year (FY)**: October 1 through September 30 for Walton County and the State of Florida.
- **Subtotal / Total row**: aggregation rows on a budget table; a Subtotal sums one category, a Total sums every category shown.
- **Scope**: whether a table reflects the whole county, one fund, or one department.
